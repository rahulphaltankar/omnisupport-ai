# Responsible-AI risk register

Likelihood (L) and Impact (I) are scored 1–5; score = L×I. "Evidence" points to the artefact proving the control exists.
Owner roles are organisational (this is a prototype): **ML** = ML engineer, **Ops** = support operations lead, **Sec** = security/privacy lead, **Eng** = platform engineer.

| # | Risk | Category | L | I | Score | Mitigation / control (implemented) | Evidence | Owner | Residual / monitoring |

| R02 | Duplicate / customer contamination across splits | Data | 3 | 4 | 12 | Exact-duplicate removal before splits; `GroupKFold` by customer in CV; overlap reported | `artifacts/data_audit/cleaning_report.csv` | ML | Medium: ~96% customer overlap is realistic but means "known customer" generalisation only |
